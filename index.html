FAQ

Questions Answered

How long does it take to go live? +

Standard automations go live within 24–48 hours after the discovery call. Complex integrations (ServiceNow, Office 365) may take 5–7 business days depending on scope and access.

Do I need any technical knowledge? +

None at all. We handle everything from server setup to deployment and maintenance. You use the end result — your bot, automated tickets, generated reports. We also train your team.

Is the WhatsApp integration via official API? +

Yes. We use the official WhatsApp Business API (Meta-approved) — ensuring policy compliance, reliable delivery and the ability to scale to thousands of contacts legally.

What kind of businesses do you work with? +

CA firms, IT companies, retail businesses, logistics firms, clinics, coaching institutes, real estate agencies and more. If you have repetitive processes, we can automate them.

Can you integrate with our existing software? +

Yes. We integrate with ServiceNow, Office 365, WhatsApp, CRMs, ERPs, Google Sheets, Tally and most other tools via APIs or RPA where direct integration isn't available.

What support do you provide after deployment? +

Every engagement includes monthly maintenance, updates, bug fixes and WhatsApp-based support. We proactively monitor your automations and alert you before issues become problems.
